# Visual production reference — eight separate photo/art directions

All photos must be properly licensed or generated by available tools, recorded with provenance. Do NOT copy competitor website shots. All are fictional concept businesses. Prefer coherent image sets per record (identical building, garment, dish or practitioner identity). Create alternative mobile crops, at least one hero image and images for all detail pages.

| Vertical | Hero shot direction | Detail gallery direction | Graphic language | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Construction | Architectural contemporary interiors under natural side light, realistic scale, amber accent | One illustrative building with consistent facade from 4 views + line-plan annotations | Section numbers, long vertical grids and material swatches | Fake construction awards, generic skyline montage |
| Industrial | Premium macro metal extrusion/machining, authentic surface textures | Product on neutral technical sweep, scale drawing with consistent unit marks | CAD-style measurement labels, precision tables | Mismatched material grades/measurements |
| Real Estate | Editorial villa/residential design dusk warm natural stone | Each unit same hypothetical apartment from living room, bedroom, kitchen and floorplan | Warm limestone, architectural floorplan cards | Misleading real property addresses/ROI |
| Dental | Safe welcoming modern consultation room with sterile tools, fictional editorial professional portraits | Treatment guides as diagrams and clinical environment, no patient before/after | Sage panels, visual care process | Real doctors copied, medical claims |
| Ecommerce | Fashion campaign with soft tactile fabric and confident garment styling | Each garment identical product across flatlay/model/detail/color images | Magazine-like spread, restrained serif | Swapped product colors and fake reviews |
| Portfolio | Bold poster/grid showing meaningful design process artefacts | Case moodboard, selected/alternative direction, end deliverables consistent with brief | Type-centered editorial poster layouts | Fake brand clients or vague AI graphics |
| Restaurant | Rustic tabletop ingredient still-life, warm natural food portrait | Same dish ingredient closeup, overhead plate, side view, menu treatment | Cinnamon ink, menu border rules | Food photo unrelated to described dish/allergens |
| Academy | Realistic collaborative studying atmosphere with original illustrative elements | Curriculum diagrams, individual lesson screenshots, instructor portrait as fictional illustration | Syllabus timeline, indigo chapters | Fake accreditation/employment guarantees |

For each hero provide prompts specifying camera, lens/angle, lighting, material palette, subjects, spatial constraints, aspect ratios, no embedded text/logo, precise fictional context, and negative prompts for AI artifacts. Inspect for distortions before use. Hero/photo production should not block compiling skeleton, but missing hero assets blocks DONE.
