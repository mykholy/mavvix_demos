# AR/EN copy, brand voice and truthfulness

- Showcase by Mavvix: Arabic Egyptian-professional (clear, warm, no overdone slang); English concise B2B. CTA: `شوف الديمو`, `اطلب موقع مشابه`, `اعرف الباقة`, `Request a similar website`.
- Demo companies are fictional concepts. State this openly. Avoid fake client approvals, ratings, testimonials, medical accreditations, ISO certifications, before-after medical claims, manufactured performance statistics, real partner company logos, guarantees, inaccurate delivery schedules.
- Use specific copy: Construction "اطّلع على نوع المشروع ومراحل التنفيذ" rather than "نبتكر المستقبل"; Industry "قارن مواصفات المنتج واطلب عرض سعر" rather than "حلول متكاملة لكل احتياجاتك".
- No lorem ipsum or ChatGPT-style filler ('revolutionize', 'elevate', 'unlock', 'seamless solutions'). Write plain expert copy that answers buyer questions.
- CTA promises accurate destination: `طلب معاينة تجريبية` in property demo instead of `تم تأكيد المعاينة` without a backend/calendar.
- Demo content volume: at least 8 project listings for Construction; 12 catalog items for Industrial; 12 property units; 5 practitioners + 8 services for Dental; 12 products with variants for Ecom; 8 portfolio projects. Rich details for at least 5 seed records per sector.
- All public-facing content should be localized (EN and AR), including URL breadcrumbs, buttons, schema validation errors, form labels and toast messages. Avoid a single language for hidden pages.
- Every fictional testimonial area must be replaced by verifiable process/how-it-works/FAQ or explicit "illustrative quote" signage (prefer avoid altogether).
- Treat images as illustrative and use alt text reflecting what's visible, not SEO stuffing. AI-generated product images must never depict different product ingredients/ingredients lists from the reference; in ecom concept, use fictional products only.
