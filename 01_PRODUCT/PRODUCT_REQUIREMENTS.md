# V2 Product Requirements — client-grade demonstrators, not mockups

## Business outcome
Convert Mavvix website design ad traffic into qualified website project inquiries by letting prospects explore a **working equivalent to an actual bespoke industry website**, with full navigation and functional journeys rather than home-only page shots. Showcase must explain the design is an illustrative fictional concept. It cannot be represented as a paying customer portfolio.

## Deliverables
- One bilingual Mavvix sales Showcase linking only verified sites, filter by vertical, accurate screenshots and tailored sales inquiry CTA.
- Eight mandatory distinctive full demos: Construction, Industrial, Real Estate, Dental, Ecommerce, Portfolio, Restaurant, Academy.
- Fully configured Node runtime SQLite app, deterministic seeds, working database-backed domain workflows, protected Content Studio admin, SEO/blog/media/branding editing, true bilingual and responsive design, tests and deployment plan.
- Every demo has 10+ meaningful route patterns, multiple populated item/details, three functional journeys including negative cases, CMS and request/order review pages, and no dead controls.
- All concepts use fictional business names and example prices/status only, explicitly labeled. Real Mavvix branding is used exclusively for showcase and subtle provider attribution.

## Measurable gates
1. 8/8 sector packages pass route and functional QA.
2. All 8 have published Arabic + English content for every required route and representative detail record.
3. No missing media critical to primary flows, broken links, fake booking confirmations, fake payments, or unresolved client-side errors.
4. Each core transactional journey results in an honest SQLite demo record plus admin view.
5. At least one edit→preview→publish→public update→rollback test per vertical.
6. Screenshots from real browser for 390px and 1440px per homepage/detail/primary workflow/admin.
7. All core unit/integration/E2E tests pass; accessibility and performance measurements recorded with real tooling.
8. Versioned checkpoint and deployment runbook.

## MVP vs full release
A staged launch can show completed Construction/Industrial/Real Estate first with an explicit '3 live previews, five in production' status. DO NOT mark eight-demo project complete until all eight meet gates. Unlike V1, Restaurant and Academy are required for the full package.

## Commercial scope
The demos show capabilities and UI, but do not automatically commit Mavvix to delivering all sample capabilities for a quoted starter price. The true customer contract must distinguish frontend theme, content management, transaction workflows, third-party integrations and support terms.
