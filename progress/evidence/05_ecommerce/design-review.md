# Ecommerce (Noria Atelier) — Design & Editorial Review

## 1. Visual Direction & Art Direction Differences
- **Palette:** Warm white `#FAF8F5`, deep ink `#1A1715`, desaturated rose `#C89B8C` / `#D4A396`, and textured stone `#EBE5DF`.
- **Atmosphere:** Modern fashion editorial atelier, tactile linen weave textures, bold editorial typography with serif headings, and expansive whitespace.
- **Distinct from Siblings:**
  - Distinct from Construction (heavy industrial slate/orange) and Industrial (steel gray/amber data tables).
  - Distinct from Real Estate (high-end architectural developments and floorplans) and Dental (calming clinical sage/teal).
  - Noria Atelier focuses on garment drape, swatch matrix interaction, dynamic cart arithmetic, and simulated COD checkout.

## 2. Integrity & Ethical Compliance
- **Simulated COD Only:** No real payment processor or credit card numbers are ever requested or stored.
- **True Variant Matrix:** 96 variants across 24 products with true stock numbers in SQLite, including 7 intentional out-of-stock variants to test failure modes.
- **Server-Side Re-Pricing:** Pricing engine calculates subtotals, deterministic shipping rates (Cairo 70 EGP, Alex 95 EGP, Other 125 EGP), and promotional discount caps.
- **Bilingual Arabic/English:** Complete editorial voice in natural Arabic and English.
