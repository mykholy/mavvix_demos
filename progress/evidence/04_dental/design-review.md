# Dental (Lumen Dental) — Design & Editorial Review

## 1. Visual Direction & Art Direction Differences
- **Palette:** Sage `#EDF5F2`, deep teal `#193A39`, soft muted clay accents, and tranquil white surfaces.
- **Atmosphere:** Deeply calming, clinical yet welcoming, sensory-friendly typography with serif headings and clean sans body.
- **Distinct from Siblings:**
  - Unlike Construction (heavy dark slate and architectural engineering lines) and Industrial (technical amber-accented precision tables), Lumen Dental uses organic rounded radii (rounded-2xl, rounded-3xl), airy whitespace, and soft teal borders.
  - Unlike Real Estate (luxury gold-rimmed high-contrast photography), Dental focuses on patient reassurance, transparent clinical steps, and zero-pressure consultation flows.

## 2. Copywriting & Ethical Compliance
- **Non-Diagnostic:** All treatments explicitly state they are illustrative consultation frameworks and do not offer automated self-diagnosis.
- **Fictional Personas:** All 6 practitioners clearly carry the disclosure that they are fictional illustrative personas for the Mavvix commercial platform kit.
- **No Real Transactions:** The appointment request wizard yields an honest, clearly labelled simulated receipt stating that no medical booking has occurred.
- **Bilingual Arabic/English:** All headings, bodies, FAQs, and buttons are crafted in professional, natural Arabic alongside fluent English.
