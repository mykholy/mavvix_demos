# 08 — NOVA LEARNING / نوفا للتعلّم — Visual & Design Review

**Vertical:** Academy & Professional Applied Learning Platform  
**Target Demos:** 8 of 8  
**Verification Date:** November 2026  
**Art Direction Mode:** Intellectual, Structured, High-Density Applied Technical Learning

---

## 1. Visual Identity & Palette Distinctiveness

| Token | Hex Value | Semantic Usage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Intellectual Deep Navy** | `#192A48` | Primary brand anchor, headers, hero typography, button primaries, footer background |
| **Soft Periwinkle Canvas** | `#F5F7FF` | Subdued platform background, badge fills, chapter rail sidebar backgrounds |
| **Applied Coral Accent** | `#EF6A55` | High-intent calls to action, active tab indicators, quiz progress bars, preview lesson pills |
| **Indigo-Slate** | `#5F67B5` | Secondary links, syllabus module headings, active filter pills, code syntax accents |
| **Pure White** | `#FFFFFF` | Structured cards, course dossier panels, quiz question blocks, elevated modals |

*Evaluation:*  
The palette creates an unmistakably academic yet modern applied-tech aesthetic, completely differentiated from the heavy steel of Ferroline Industries, the saffron parchment of Saffron Table, the architectural neutrals of Meridian Residences, or the clinical sage of Lumen Dental.

---

## 2. Typography & Editorial Density

- **Headings:** Classical high-contrast serif font family (`font-serif`), conveying scholarly rigor and intellectual authority.
- **Body & Controls:** High-legibility modern sans-serif (`font-sans`), with tight spatial leading, clean numeric alignments, and monospace code blocks for lab checklists.
- **Substantive Curriculum Content:**
  - 12 comprehensive curricula across Data, Engineering, UX, and Marketing.
  - 72 seeded lessons with individual lesson transcripts exceeding 400 words in both Arabic and English.
  - 6 substantive articles exceeding 200–240 words each across 3 structured sections with table of contents.
  - 4-question deterministic quiz with detailed technical rationale boxes.

---

## 3. Bilingual (RTL / LTR) Integrity

- **RTL Support:** Complete mirroring across breadcrumbs, chapter rails, filter pills, question counters, radio choices, and next/prev buttons.
- **Micro-copy:** Carefully adapted Arabic terminology (e.g., "نظم التصميم المعيارية", "التحقق عند حدود النظام", "مخطط الصندوق والطرفين") avoiding mechanical literal translations.
- **Language Switcher:** Seamless instantaneous route switching between `/[locale]/preview/academy/...` preserving deep route hierarchy and slugs.

---

## 4. Ethical Transparency & Consumer Trust

- Unobtrusive persistent demo notice at the top of every screen:
  *"Interactive Academy Concept Demo — Nova Learning by Mavvix (Scored Quizzes & SQLite Progress Persistence)"*.
- Explicit disclosures across all course dossiers, instructor directories, and receipt pages clarifying that certifications are simulated, non-accredited, and free of real tuition charges.
- Zero fabricate placement promises or deceptive university accreditation claims.
