### Positioning hypothesis
- Target Audience: Venture capital partners, boutique luxury hospitality founders, cultural institutions, architecture practices, and enterprise founders seeking bespoke brand systems.
- Core Values: Typographic restraint, architectural grid discipline, transparent deliverable inventories, and explicit decision rationale.
- Palette: 60% warm paper off-white (`#F6F5F2`), 30% deep graphite/black (`#0F0F10`), 10% electric lime accent (`#CCFF00`) with architectural parchment grays (`#E2E0D8`, `#57544C`, `#737068`).
- Contrast Standard: All text strictly complies with WCAG AA (4.5:1 minimum). In dark backgrounds (`#0F0F10`), text uses `#E5E3DD` or `text-slate-300`, badges use `#CCFF00` on dark or deep ink on `#CCFF00`.

### Factual and trust safeguards
No fabricated real-world client testimonials or false metrics. All case studies clearly state their conceptual and demonstrative nature. Project inquiries are persisted directly to SQLite and marked `simulated` with a distinctive concept demo notification.
