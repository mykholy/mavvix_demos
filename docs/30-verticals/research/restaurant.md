### Positioning hypothesis
- Target Audience: Families, business dining, culinary tourists, and evening gatherings seeking unhurried authentic Middle Eastern gastronomy in distinguished heritage architectural surroundings.
- Visual Palette: 60% warm cream `#FAF7F2` / `#F6F0E4`, 30% deep rich olive/charcoal (`#2B3627`, `#1C1917`), 10% burnt cinnamon/saffron (`#8F3E29` / `#C26733`).
- Contrast Standard: Strictly adheres to WCAG AA (4.5:1 minimum). On dark surfaces, badges use `#F2B38E` or `#D9D5A2` with `text-slate-200`; on light surfaces, labels use `#4A2318` or `#2B3627`.
- Accessibility: All interactive modifiers, reservation date pickers, and dietary filter buttons include explicit `aria-label`s and visible focus states.

### Factual and trust safeguards
No fabricated real-world client reviews or Michelin guide claims. All dish preparations are presented as authentic culinary traditions. Orders and reservations are persisted directly to SQLite and marked `simulated` with an explicit concept demo disclaimer.
