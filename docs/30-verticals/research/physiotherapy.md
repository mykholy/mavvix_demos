- Target Audience: Patients recovering from orthopedic injuries, athletes seeking safe return-to-sport progression, professionals managing desk-bound postural strain, and individuals needing structured mobility retraining in Egypt, UAE, and Saudi Arabia.
- Visual Palette: 60% fresh therapeutic sage / porcelain mint (`#E9F4EC`, `#F5FCF4`, `#FCFDF8`), 30% deep forest teal / botanical pine (`#1D3934`, `#14352E`, `#203F38`), 10% active kinetic emerald (`#287459`, `#2D826C`) with high-contrast text (`#14352E`, `#1E4037`, `#3F6659`).

- Accessibility: All functional goal selectors, appointment preference inputs, and accordion tabs include explicit `aria-label`s, `id`/`htmlFor` pairings, and visible focus states.

### Factual and trust safeguards
Zero fabricated patient testimonials, fake before/after imagery, or guaranteed cure promises. All movement modules are described as structured rehabilitation pathways governed by licensed clinical oversight. Sample appointment enquiries collect only broad functional categories and administrative timing preferences, rigorously omitting sensitive medical history or diagnoses.
