# BloomSteps — Nursery website research (2026-11-24)

## Observed first-party comparisons
- HEI Schools Egypt — https://www.heischoolsegypt.com/admissions-page : explains campus visits, age groups, play-based philosophy and capacity-dependent enrollment; fees discussed in the admissions process.
- Happy Doodles Egypt — https://happydoodlesegypt.com/admissions.html : visit request and admissions/fees, daily program and parent communication. Do not copy fee or claims into BloomSteps.
- RS Nurseries, Saudi Arabia — https://rsg.edu.sa/nurseries/admissions/ and https://rsg.edu.sa/nurseries/faq/ : visit first, real registration and required records afterwards; distinguishes activity routines, program and parent checklist.
- Kids Home Preschool Riyadh — https://kidshome.sa/ : invites a visit to understand pedagogy, policies and learning setting.

## Opportunity
For a branded nursery site, show age-band differences, daily routine, parent concerns, a safeguarding checklist and a simple guardian visit enquiry. Keep detailed child's records outside the public funnel. Egypt query hypotheses: حضانة قريبة, زيارة حضانة, نظام اليوم في الحضانة; GCC: حضانة أطفال الرياض, زيارة حضانة, برامج الطفولة المبكرة. No search volume or ads verified.

Qualitative score: urgency 4.0, differentiation 4.4, reuse 4.1, deal value 3.7, reachability 4.4 (weighted 30/25/20/15/10): 4.12/5; not an empirical measure.

## Art direction and constraints
Teal ink #145960, marigold #F5C04E, warm ivory, bright illustrated geometric motifs, readable rounded typography and real photographs of teaching materials (not identifiable children). Brand, age bands, routines and curriculum entries are illustrative. Never claim real accreditation, licensing, staff, safeguarding compliance, live spaces or confirmed admission. Strict intake stores age band, selected programme interest, region, date window and guardian contact only; no child name/DOB, photos, medical details, documents, address, or free-form sensitive information. Before a live client handoff, owner must supply approved actual policies, ages and operators.
