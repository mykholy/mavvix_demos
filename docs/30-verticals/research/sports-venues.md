## Differentiated Mavvix product brief

**Brand:** Arena Courts. **Art direction:** stadium-after-dark / ink-pine #10231F, chalk white #E9F4EE, amber floodlight #F0AB47, electric mint #B6FD74. Bold compressed athletic display, industrial scoreboard labels, large real floodlit court photos, large typographic time selectors, strong Arabic headings. Unlike dark motorsport Torque, Arena is a pure sports scorecard/site schedule identity.

**User journey** (persistent, nonbinding): choose sport (football/padel), exact court profile, participants (football 10–14; padel 4), date 1–60 days ahead, selected start hour and duration (football 60–120 mins; padel 60/90 mins), skill or session type, consent/name/contact, store pending request in `portfolio_requests`, return reference `MVX-ARN-...`. No live slot promises; "requested time is a preference, pending venue confirmation."
