### Positioning hypothesis
- Egypt phrases: “ديتيلنج سيارات”, “تلميع السيارة”, “غسيل داخلي للسيارات”, “نانو سيراميك”, “تلميع فوانيس”. KSA: “تلميع سيارات”, “حماية الطلاء PPF”, “عناية داخلية للسيارة”, “تجديد طلاء السيارة”. Search interest is unmeasured.
- Buyer objections: What is included? How do small hatchback and large SUVs differ? Will paint correction remove any scratch? How long will a ceramic coat last? Is the trunk/engine bay included? Does the quote cover seats, roof lining and fabric? Are there real dates or just enquiry preferences?
- Strategy: a visually premium **black / ice-blue** GlossBay brand with an original fluorescent wash-bay style, macro texture and a tactile “vehicle-to-treatment” quote builder. This is NOT Torque Garage's industrial orange diagnostics design.
- Hypothetical qualitative score 4.15/5 (urgency 4, specialization 4.5, reuse 4.5, plausible project value 3.5, reachable owners 4), a judgment not a numerical market fact.

### Factual and trust safeguards
No invented studio address, after/before results, warranty, product brands or prices. Ceramic coating is not scratch proof, PPF application and coating durability depend on approved products, care and real inspection. A request stores choice, location and contact only; response is **pending operator confirmation** and does not reserve real capacity. Never claim a photo shows a GlossBay client or finished job.
