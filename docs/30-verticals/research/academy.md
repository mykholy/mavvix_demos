### Positioning hypothesis
- Target Audience: Mid-level engineers, career switchers, computer science students, and corporate engineering teams in Egypt, Saudi Arabia, and UAE seeking practical mastery of production-grade architectures.
- Visual Palette: 60% deep technical navy/slate (`#121D33`, `#192A48`), 30% clean architectural paper (`#F5F7FF`, `#FFFFFF`), 10% energetic terracotta coral (`#B74132` / `#D94E34`).
- Contrast Standard: Strictly adheres to WCAG AA (4.5:1 minimum). On dark surfaces, text uses `#FFFFFF`, `#F5F7FF`, or `#D9D5A2`; on light surfaces, text uses `#121D33` or `#192A48`. All buttons use verified high-contrast combinations.
- Accessibility: All interactive curriculum selectors, quiz options, and enrollment forms include explicit `aria-label`s, `id`/`htmlFor` pairings, and visible focus states.

### Factual and trust safeguards
No fake student testimonials, fabricated job placement guarantees, or unauthorized university accreditation claims. All learning tracks are described as rigorous skill development curricula. Student quiz submissions and course progress are persisted locally to SQLite and marked with an unobtrusive concept demo disclosure.
