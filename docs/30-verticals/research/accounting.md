## Brand and buyer journey
- Name: LedgerPoint; palette `#1C3343` dark slate, `#50C2C3` aqua, `#F0EEE8` soft paper. Independent professional typography: IBM Plex Sans Condensed for EN, Cairo for AR, tabular numeric presentations rather than another legal/editorial journal layout.
- Audience: real Egypt/Saudi SMEs with recurring transaction entry, invoicing, reconciliation, payroll, tax or monthly management reporting needs. Clear difference between a custom marketing site and a complete accounting ERP.
- At least eight substantive scoped services: bookkeeping, reconciliation, monthly management reporting, payroll processing, VAT/Zakat orientation (not advice), e-invoice preparation, startup books setup, control/audit-readiness support.
- Enquiry: country preference (Egypt/KSA/other), entity type, service scope and monthly invoice volume band, current accounting workflow, desired service cadence, business contact with consent; **no tax IDs, bank statements, financial records, revenue amounts, employee identifiers or confidential documents**.
- Pending-only reference `MVX-LED-*` saved via dedicated API with strict Zod validation; authenticated admin queue; no automatically calculated tax or legally binding quote.
- 3 authored educational articles, 5 FAQs, 9+ homepage blocks with 4 accounting-only topics, 7+ inner families and true desktop/mobile responsive interface.
