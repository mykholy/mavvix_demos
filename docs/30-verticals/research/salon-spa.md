## Client discovery
Egyptian buying-language hypotheses: صالون تجميل موقع حجز, حجز ميك اب, صالون اظافر, باقات عرايس. Saudi: صالون نسائي حجز, صالون تجميل خدمة شعر, باقات سبا. No verified search volume, CPL, conversion percentages or Meta Ad IDs.

Distinct creative direction: **quiet luxury rosewood/porcelain** vs prior Carbon Orange Torque and Cobalt Mustard Fixora. Large magazine-serif English headlines, IBM Plex Arabic display typography, generous negative space, offset portrait/photo crop, botanical linework, subtle gold separators. Hero headline sells the *care ritual*, while upper and lower contextual notice makes clear Velora is a Mavvix concept.

Full enquiry: one or more selected services, exact service duration from authored allowable options, **provider speciality preference only** (not fabricated individual staff), requested date, broad time window, consent and contact. Customer sees pending request with reference; no confirmed salon slot, no live price, no payment.

Value hypothesis 0–5 with weights urgency 30%, differentiation 25%, reuse 20%, deal value 15%, reachability 10%: 4.1×.3 + 4.3×.25 + 4×.2 + 3.8×.15 + 4.5×.1 = **4.125/5**, qualitative only.

## Client data / trust constraints
- No staff biographies or credentials without owner approval; present roles as hypothetical categories.
- Service durations are **illustrative** and need final operator review; never exact bookable slots.
- No before/after images, treatments results, client testimonials, public contact numbers or addresses invented.
- Never imply dermatology/clinical care; spa/wellbeing is cosmetic only.
- Do not collect health conditions, allergy details or skin/medical photos through the example form.
