## Distinct design direction
Pulse District: editorial kinetic typography + disciplined charcoal/violet/lime, NOT red/yellow/blue and NOT glossy salon. Graphite #141421; purple #8E6BFF; acidic lime #E0FE61; warm neutral #F2F2ED. Bold all-caps condensed Latin heading with IBM Plex Arabic or Tajawal Arabic strong geometric type; large asymmetric staggered stats as *training concepts* only, **no fabricated member count/results**.
- Hero authentic exercise-equipment photograph with verifiable license; never present as our owned gym.
- Sector-specific blocks: class-type matrix (strength/conditioning/mobility), beginner readiness path, choose training rhythm (2,3,4 day preference, NOT personalized medical plan), coach specialism (role-only; no imaginary trainers), sample weekly timetable marked **illustrative and not live availability**, non-binding membership tier comparison with no unverified actual prices.
- Visitor flow: desired program + experience level + goal category + trial/class preference + date window + general region + consented contact, pending-only request with `MVX-PUL-*`, no completed enrollment or membership charge.
- Identity/copy must make clear not an operating gym or provider with real coaches, addresses, member outcomes or verified prices. No health, injury, condition, weight or body measurements in the enquiry form.
- Three guides: `choosing-class`, `first-visit-prep`, `training-frequency`. Keep basic guidance educational and refer individuals to suitable professionals for exercise contraindications.
