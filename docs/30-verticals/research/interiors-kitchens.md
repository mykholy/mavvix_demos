## Differentiation / sales hypothesis
Egyptian queries: `تصميم مطبخ حرف L`, `تفصيل مطابخ حسب المقاس`, `قياس مطبخ قبل التصميم`, `تصميم غرفة دريسنج`.
Saudi/GCC: `تصميم مطبخ مودرن حسب المقاس`, `مطابخ حرف U`, `استشارة تصميم مطابخ`.
Search volume, conversion rates and CPC: **not measured**.

Identity **FORMA ATELIER**: architectural drafting meets warm interior publication. Ink `#242526`, parchment `#F3EEE7`, deep oxblood `#6C3538`, brass-clay accent `#B9866B`. Distinct from Oakline's furniture showroom/editorial product grid: architectural wall-coordinate SVG, long-form layout descriptions, modular horizontal bands, ink-and-clay CTA, Fraunces English editorial and Tajawal Arabic. Do not reuse generic card rhythms.

Core transaction: `space type + kitchen layout + wall lengths (mm) + island preference + cabinet face material + countertop + budget band + target timing -> nonbinding design-consultation request`. No instant binding quote, 3D rendering claims, actual manufacturer warranty or scheduling guarantee. The server validates project scope and minimum/maximum dimensions, makes a `MVX-FRM-*` idempotent request, stores consent/source without a full home address, and staff reviews it in existing protected queue.
