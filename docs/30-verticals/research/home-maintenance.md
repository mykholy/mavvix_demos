Status: research only; site is not implemented or marked live. The full master plan assigns this sector to Wave 1 immediately after Auto Service.

## Differentiated sales journey
- Sector art direction: Fixora Care / clean modular white surfaces, cobalt ink (`#174E78`), mustard accent (`#F7B347`) and infographic-like service categories. **Not** the automotive site's dark motorsport editorial treatment.
- The visitor selects category (AC/plumbing/electrical/appliance/carpentry/etc.), symptom, **safe urgency** (not claim real emergency dispatch), neighborhood/service area without street or access codes, preferred day/time, and optional recurring maintenance frequency.
- Server checks approved category and supported severity values, validates nonbinding date and consent; saves an isolated `portfolio_request`, returns reference `MVX-HM-*`. No exact address, images of real customer homes, diagnosis or dispatch confirmation.
- At least eight original service dossiers, dedicated sections for coverage, planned visits, seasonal A/C upkeep, water/leak safety and escalation notes, service guide, booking enquiry and branch-free contact.
- Domain content needs care: major electric shock/gas emergencies should not instruct DIY troubleshooting. Direct users to qualified local emergency services as appropriate.
