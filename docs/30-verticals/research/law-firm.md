## Differentiated product experience
- Identity: Lexora Counsel, deep aubergine **#251D34**, warm porcelain **#FAF7F1**, brass **#B79B6A**; expressive but restrained **Fraunces (English)** and **Tajawal (Arabic)**, carefully composed manuscript-grid headings and editorial typographic flourishes. Different from PawWell soft pet shapes or the existing corporate industrial site.
- 8+ meaningful service dossiers: company formation and governance, commercial agreements, employment compliance, commercial disputes, IP/trademarks, real estate transactions, succession/family-asset planning, cross-border business advisory. Legal facts cannot be fabricated or stated as jurisdiction-neutral legal advice. Each dossier must clearly distinguish general process education from a regulated practitioner service.
- 3 original educational articles per locale with legally safe high-level process explanations and no current statute conclusions without sourced jurisdiction/legal review.
- A **conflict-aware, confidentiality-minimal** lead form: area of practice, Egypt/KSA jurisdiction preference, prospective client type (person/company), general preferred contact channel and time window, minimal name/phone, explicit consent. **No adverse-party name, confidential dispute facts, documents, case IDs, deadlines, legal questions, national ID or personal data about other parties.**
- Form states prominently: submitting does not create an attorney-client relationship, representation, privilege or consultation agreement. No fee quote or appointment confirmation. Save `pending_operator_confirmation` to isolated SQLite with idempotency and admin RBAC; file uploads disabled.
- Owner licensing, staff bios, credentials, physical office addresses, testimonials, court-case claims and fees must be verified and explicitly provided by a real firm for commercial deployment.
- SEO: intent hypotheses (not actual search volumes) `مكتب محاماة للشركات`, `صياغة عقود تجارية`, `محامي شركات مصر`, `استشارات قانونية للشركات السعودية`. Do not mark sample firm as real with structured data business address/review markup.
- Avoid automated customized legal advice; link to a real independent professional for actual instructions and urgent filing deadlines.
