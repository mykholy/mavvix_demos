# Luma Events — comparator research

Reviewed 2026-11-25. Public operator websites are evidence of positioning and information architecture, not independently verified performance. Luma is a fictional portfolio concept; no affiliation or client claims.

| Operator / source | Observed approach | Original Luma response |
|---|---|---|
| [Event House](https://ehegypt.com/) | Service categories and project-led portfolio; brief CTA | Eight explicitly conceptual experience dossiers with deliverables, constraints and planning questions; no borrowed client names or testimonials. |
| [Leapfrog](https://www.leapfrog.com.eg/) | Event management, fabrication and activation capabilities; editorial content and quote CTA | Separate production and planning routes, scope boundaries and practical field guides. |
| [Hertz](https://www.hertz-advertising.com/services/event-management) | Venue sourcing, registration, AV, coordination and post-event analysis; FAQ explains quote variability | Structured venue brief, guest journey, production milestones and pending-only enquiry; no fixed prices or availability promises. |

## Design and conversion decisions
- Editorial plum/champagne/cream palette, asymmetric photography, readable Arabic typography and mirrored navigation; avoid copying competitor layouts.
- Lead with event objective and format; show concept dossiers, scope comparison, guest journey, production phases, venue checklist, guides and FAQs before enquiry.
- Request only minimal contact and planning data; use allowlisted event type, guest and budget bands, date preference, venue status and consent. No attendee lists, payment or identity documents.
- Quote submission is a pending request, not a reservation. Explain what happens next and preserve UTM and idempotency.
- Use independently licensed photographs only; competitor images, logos and claims are excluded. Media provenance and actual byte validation remain pending.
- Acceptance: AR/EN route parity, keyboard navigation, contrast, mobile/tablet overflow, persistence, duplicate submission and protected admin tests; full existing-site regression.
